About Bonava Limited

A Kenyan supply company built around getting orders right.

Founded in May 2024 and registered in Kenya (company no. PVT-PJU2GAK5), Bonava Limited is a Nairobi-based supplier of ICT equipment and construction materials. We work with procurement teams, contractors and business owners who need the correct items, at a fair price, delivered when promised.

We keep it simple: you tell us what you need, we source it from trusted distributors and manufacturers, and we deliver it with the documentation your stores and finance teams expect.

  • Supply of goods against LPOs, RFQs and framework contracts
  • Single items through to full bills of quantities
  • Installation and after-sales support on ICT equipment
